## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional hospital and clinic queue systems often require patients to wait physically for long periods without knowing their position in the queue.

This results in:

- ⏳ Long patient waiting times
- 👥 Crowded waiting areas
- 📋 Manual queue management
- ❌ Poor visibility of doctor availability
- 🔄 Difficulty managing appointments and patient flow

### 💡 Our Solution

**MedQ Care** provides a centralized digital system where patients can:

- Register and login
- Book appointments
- Join a digital queue
- Track their queue position
- View doctor information
- Receive queue updates

Doctors and hospital staff can manage the queue through dedicated dashboards.
